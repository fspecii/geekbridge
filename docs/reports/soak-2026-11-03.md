# Soak of the real service, 2026-11-03

A 67-minute soak of the release `geekbridge-service` binary at main `a8ccb280c` in a sandbox, with a stand-in phone
driving it. Verdict: **no leak**. Descriptors, threads, state size and log size are flat; RSS rises during warm-up
and then levels off (watch item below). One unexplained, transient control-API stall in the first 2.5 minutes is
reported below as an anomaly.

## What ran

| | |
|---|---|
| Service | `target/release/geekbridge-service`, `--state-dir` private under `~/.cache/gb-tmp`, scratch `HOME` / `XDG_*`, `GEEKBRIDGE_CLIPBOARD=memory`, no tray, no D-Bus, `--dev-loopback`, `--no-announce`, own random ports (never 1716 / 17381 / 8000), environment cleared |
| Driver | `crates/bridge-service/tests/soak_real.rs` (ignored test), started by `scripts/soak-real.sh`; verdict by `scripts/soak-fit.py` |
| Stand-in phone | genuine `bridge_kde` endpoints (fresh identity per pairing) behind the `bridge-chaos` `FaultProxy`; declares every packet family the service accepts |
| Wall time | 05:01 to 06:09 local: 67.2 min of load, then 20 s quiet and 4 idle samples; one sample every 30 s (139 rows) |
| Machine | 24 cores, other agents building and running e2e at the same time; `df` of `/home` before 810 G used / 107 G free, after 809 G / 110 G (scratch deleted) |

Load per round (every 2.5 to 5 s): a burst of battery, notification (post, sometimes cancel), SMS, call-state
(ring then idle) and clipboard packets from the phone, `ClipboardPush` (and every third round `ClipboardPull`) on the
desktop, a phone-to-desktop file offer (50 KB to 3 MB, every 4th round), a desktop-to-phone `ShareSendFile`
(20 KB to 2 MB, every 5th), a fault profile plus reconnect of the persistent phone (every 4th), a pair then
unpair of a fresh identity (every 7th, alternately desktop-initiated and phone-initiated, a third of them through a
faulty link), and status / events / share / clipboard / notifications polling every 0.5 s. Fault profiles: clean,
latency plus one-byte fragmentation, bandwidth cap, loss penalty, random connection drop, reset after N bytes,
slow handshake, half-open blackhole.

**VERDICT: PASS**

## Findings

1. **No leak.** FDs 13 to 29 under load, back to 14 at idle (first sample 16); threads 3 to 20, back to 4; both
   fits have zero slope. State dir: 12 files at start, 13 to 14 after, 3.4 to 7.2 KB, far under the 40 file / 256 KB
   cap (the documented file set of `docs/state-formats.md` plus `.bak` / `.lock`); the step from 3.4 to 6.4 KB is the
   one left-over pairing described below (a certificate plus its `.bak`), not growth. The event ring sits at 128
   (cap 256), mirrored notifications at 0 to 3, share transfers at 0 to 4. The log did not grow.
2. **RSS warm-up (watch item).** 6.9 MB at start, 8.3 / 8.6 / 8.8 / 9.2 / 9.2 / 9.0 MB mean over successive 10-minute
   blocks, end 8.7 MB, high-water 9.5 MB. Theil-Sen over the whole window is +1.27 MB/h (tau 0.76), inside the
   3 MB budget, and +0.2 to 0.4 MB/h over the second half (OLS 0.22, Theil-Sen 0.39). The likely cause is the code
   pages that `memtrim` drops 2 s after start faulting back in as paths are first used, plus allocator high water;
   it was levelling off but 67 minutes cannot prove it. A 6 to 8 hour run (`SECS=28800 scripts/soak-real.sh`) would
   settle it. No code change made: nothing points at a specific retention.
3. **Wake-ups and CPU.** Under this load 19.5 voluntary context switches per second (median, p95 47.5) and 0.8% of
   one core (p95 1.8%), neither trending. Idle, driver stopped, one phone linked: 0.3 wake-ups per second, 0.02% CPU.
4. **Anomaly: transient control-API stall, not explained.** 13 of the 7675 polls timed out (4 s read timeout, no
   hello) or were refused, in two windows of about 30 s (35 to 69 s and 105 to 134 s after start), both during
   the pairing of a fresh identity (the service's trust record for the first one is stamped at the start of the first
   window). Both ended when the driver's pairing attempt gave up. A pairing that cost 32 s and 30 s instead of 0.7 s
   went with each, and the first one was not cleaned up, so the service ended with two paired devices (the driver's
   `Unpair` fell into the stall; the driver now retries it). It was not seen again in the remaining 65 minutes (68 further
   pairings), in 10 later 100 to 150 s runs of the same driver, or when the service dialed a peer that accepts and never
   answers (`geekbridge-ctl status` stayed prompt). A 250 ms delay on every `fsync` (LD_PRELOAD shim) made each pairing
   take 5 s but did not stall the API. The service runs two async worker threads, so one cause that fits is
   synchronous trust-store I/O (flock plus fsync) on a worker while the disk was busy with other agents' builds, but
   that is a hypothesis, not a finding. If it recurs on a real machine, run `scripts/soak-real.sh` with the machine
   quiet and look at `poll-errors.log` in the result directory.
5. **Not exercised.** Desktop notification banners (no session bus by design), the real clipboard tools (memory
   backend), the tray, `geekbridge-ctl` (the control protocol is driven directly), contacts / calendar phone data, and
   the emulator-based Android side (`scripts/soak-e2e.sh`, `scripts/scale-e2e.sh`, which need an AVD). The
   `bridge-phone-sim` crate only simulates calls over D-Bus and was not needed.

## Reproduce

    python3 -I scripts/soak-fit.py samples.csv --events events.tsv --poll-errors poll-errors.log
