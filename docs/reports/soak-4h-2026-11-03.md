# Four-hour soak of the real service, 2026-11-03

`SECS=14400 scripts/soak-real.sh` on a clean clone of main at `f2a29e178` (release build, sandboxed service and
stand-in phone exactly as in `soak-2026-11-03.md`, ports and state never the live ones). Run 06:23 start, 242.5 min
of load, 4 idle samples. `/home` before 811 G used / 108 G free, after 825 G / 94 G (other agents also write there;
scratch deleted). The driver does not sample `smaps_rollup`, so a sidecar read `/proc/PID/smaps_rollup` every 60 s
(Rss, Pss, Private_Dirty, Private_Clean, Anonymous).

Verdict: **no linear leak shown; one open watch item (slow anonymous-memory creep that is decelerating) and one
FAIL from the script on control-API poll failures that I could not explain.**

## RSS over the last three hours, and is it a leak

RSS is a poor signal here: `memtrim` drops idle code pages (`codepages::drop_idle`) and they refault, which shows up
as +-1.5 MB sawtooth in RSS and Private_Clean (2.6 to 4.1 MB) without any heap change. Over the last 3 h the driver's
RSS OLS slope is +38 KiB/h (Theil-Sen +204), the sidecar's is +444 KiB/h; the difference is only where the window
starts relative to a peak. The honest series is **Anonymous = Private_Dirty** (heap and stacks), which is unaffected
by code-page refault and moves one-to-one with Pss:

| minutes into run | 0-20 | 40-60 | 80-100 | 120-140 | 160-180 | 200-220 | 240 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anonymous, mean KiB | 2059 | 2427 | 3354 | 3731 | 3547 | 4011 | 4136 |

Slopes of Anonymous per hour of the run: hour 2 +1022 KiB/h, hour 3 -181, hour 4 +367 KiB/h (last 3 h: +338, last
30 min +333). A fit of `a + b ln t` over t >= 10 min gives b = 751 KiB per e-fold, implying about 190 KiB/h now and
about 60 KiB/h at 12 h. It dips at 140 min (a trim) and the final readings (4011, 4099, 4136) are close to flat.

Reading: the creep is real but small (+2.1 MB in four hours, from a 7 to 10 MB process) and decelerating, under a
heavy churn of 340 pairings and 1.5 GB of transfers; that fits glibc arena fragmentation and caches growing to their
working-set size rather than an unbounded collection. It does not prove saturation: a linear leak of about 300 KiB/h
would be indistinguishable from this tail on four hours. All bounded structures hold (events at their cap 128/256,
state files and bytes flat, log flat, fds and threads flat), so no candidate for a per-pair or per-packet leak was
found and no code change was made. Recommended if it matters: a 12 h run on the same sidecar, or an
`LD_PRELOAD`/heaptrack run of the soak; below ~100 KiB/h at 12 h should be treated as closed.

## Anomaly: control-API poll failures (script verdict FAIL)

151 of 26669 polls failed (119 after minute 10); 144 were the 4 s read timeout (`WouldBlock`), the rest
`service closed the connection`. They are in 24 clusters, 140 in the first 90 min (37 / 62 / 41 per 30 min), then 7,
4, and none after 8891 s. They are not tied to memory, fds or threads (flat), and are over-represented only mildly
around reconnects under `latency+fragment` / `reset-after-bytes` / `loss` profiles. The service log has no
warnings. The machine was shared with other agents' builds and e2e runs, whose load I could not reconstruct, and the
first soak saw the same kind of stall (13 polls, first 135 s). A stall of the control API of 4 s with an idle service
is unexplained; it is not a regression (the first report already flagged it) and no fix was made. Next step: have
the driver record the service's own `/proc/PID/stat` state and host load average when a poll times out.
